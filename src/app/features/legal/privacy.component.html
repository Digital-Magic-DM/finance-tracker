Zero Data Selling Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal or financial records to third-party advertisers, brokerages, or data aggregators. Your records are utilized strictly to compute your private portfolio analytics.

// SECTION 01

1. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles and collect only the records necessary to calculate accurate net worth and financial runway metrics:

  • → Authentication Credentials: When signing in via Google OAuth or Email credentials, we receive your primary email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) provisioned by Firebase Auth.
  • → User-Submitted Financial Entries: Asset holdings, tickers, quantities, purchase prices, transaction ledger records, category allocations, and optional user notes entered or imported.
  • → Profile Planning Parameters: Optional user-configured parameters such as target retirement multipliers, emergency fund runway targets, and insurance coverage figures.
  • → Client-Side Preferences: Interface settings (such as dark/light mode and table display states) saved locally on your device via localStorage.
// SECTION 02

2. Processing & Calculation Functions

Your records are processed strictly for the following operational features:

  • → Computing overall net worth, asset class diversification, and unrealized profit/loss.
  • → Projecting emergency runway duration and Financial Independence (FIRE) milestones.
  • → Querying indicative market prices for your designated asset symbols.
  • → Cryptographically authenticating sessions to enforce single-user access controls.
// SECTION 03

3. Client-Side Document & Statement Parsing

When you import broker statements (such as Zerodha, Groww, INDmoney, or CDSL/NSDL CAS statements), document text parsing is performed client-side directly within your browser sandbox. Your raw statement files (PDFs/CSVs) are parsed into asset records and are never retained or archived on external servers.

// SECTION 04

4. Data Storage & Security Architecture

We implement multi-layered industry safeguards to protect your records:

  • → Encryption in Transit & At Rest: All network communication is strictly enforced over TLS 1.3 (HTTPS). Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • → Granular Access Rules: Firestore security rules enforce strict cryptographic token checks; records under /users/{uid} can only be read, modified, or deleted by the verified owner.
// SECTION 05

5. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We work with trusted global infrastructure providers to deliver high availability:

  • → Google Firebase (Alphabet Inc.): Supplies authentication, database storage, and edge static hosting under rigorous global compliance certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2).
  • → Market Quote Feeds (MFAPI, Google Finance): When fetching quote updates, only public symbol codes are transmitted. Your identity, net worth, and quantity held are never shared.
// SECTION 06

6. User Rights & Data Portability

You maintain full sovereign control over your portfolio data:

  • → Full Access & Modification: You can inspect, modify, or correct any asset holding or transaction at any time.
  • → Universal Data Export: You can export your entire portfolio and transaction ledger to CSV/Excel at any time.
  • → Permanent Deletion: You can delete individual entries or request complete account erasure.
// SECTION 07

7. Cookies & Local Storage

FinTracker does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial marketing pixels. Browser storage (localStorage and session tokens) is utilized solely to maintain your authenticated session and preserve interface preferences.

// SECTION 08

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to reflect platform additions or legal requirements. The "Last Updated" indicator at the top reflects the active version date. Continued usage of FinTracker signifies acknowledgment of revisions.
